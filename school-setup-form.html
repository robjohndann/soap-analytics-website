Two-step verification

When required, everyone who signs in to this school is asked for a code from an authenticator app (and is helped to set one up the first time). Changing this setting takes effect immediately — it does not depend on Save School Setup — and always needs a fresh authenticator code from you.

Viewer Tracker

Viewers can log in to view this school's published SOAPs. They cannot enter or edit data, and are separate from the Responsible Persons and Additional Contributors above. You can also add a Contributor here: they keep their Contributor access and sections and gain Viewer access to the published reports. Adding or removing a Viewer takes effect immediately — it does not depend on Save School Setup.
